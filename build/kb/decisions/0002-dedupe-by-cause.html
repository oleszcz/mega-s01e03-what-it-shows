0002 · Confirm the cause before merging reports

owner oleszcz with OpenCode accepted reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

Date: 29 October 2026 · Replaces: decision 0001.

Context

The old subject-only rule hid separate causes in #4471. Its prose disagrees with the desk’s computed cause table and interactive duplicate explorer. See the signed incident review for the evidence and tickets.

Decision

Read the report and look up its tenant locale, booking form, and channel. Merge only when the mechanism and affected path establish the same cause. A matching subject or intake tag does not prove a duplicate. Different subjects can still share a cause. Unknown tenants and unexplained symptoms stay open for investigation; do not force them into a known bucket.

Consequences

Triage needs more reading, but engineering receives separate actionable causes. Preserve unique report IDs and the original evidence. Reconcile counts after each export, and keep anomalies visible even when the automatic classifier assigns them to an existing cause. The historical accepted record 0001 remains unchanged.
